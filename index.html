About Doldam Hospital

Established in 2005, Doldam Hospital has grown into a premier healthcare institution known for its patient-first approach and cutting-edge medical technology.

We offer comprehensive medical services across 30+ specialties, with a dedicated team of over 150 doctors and 500 support staff.

Accredited by NABH and certified with ISO 9001:2024, we continuously strive for excellence in clinical outcomes and patient satisfaction.
